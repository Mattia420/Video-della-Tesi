About me

A few lines about me, even more important than my projects.

I work across the full spectrum of visual communication: graphic design, animation, video editing and video mapping are the skills I've built my path on, between technical study and real-world projects.

In recent years I've paired these tools with artificial intelligence, which I use to speed up my workflow and expand creative possibilities, while always keeping creative direction and control over the final result. I don't choose between craft and AI: I combine them.
